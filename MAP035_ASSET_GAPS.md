## HIGH VALUE

- Additional rural building silhouettes/footprints: both roadside rows visibly
  repeat one identical roof. Quarter turns preserve scale but do not supply town
  variety. A small compatible set would improve this actual settlement.
- Authored unpaved access/yard treatment: doors point toward sensible grass yards,
  but nothing visually connects the houses to the paved corridor. Current grass
  yards make the settlement look like a row of placed objects.
- Field/meadow or cultivated-ground treatment with compatible transitions: most
  of the kilometer-scale overview remains a single grass family. Groves alone do
  not give the large empty spaces a rural land-use structure. The next composition
  task would need placement rules as well as assets; PNG variety alone cannot fix
  the sparse sector.

## PRODUCTION LATER

- Road straight/corner shoulder and marking continuity: the Local truck-bend
  capture exposes a visible shoulder-width step and marking transition at the
  supplied join, despite metadata ports, physical pavement and tangents matching.
  The truck fits. Preserve the current provisional road decision; no seam cover,
  repaint or source adjustment was used.
- Additional tree canopy variation: clustered placements read as groves, but the
  same small canopy repeats. More species/size silhouettes could help later;
  nonuniform stretching is not a replacement for authored variation.
- House façade/roof cue and road-marking finish remain their previously accepted
  provisional quality. This scene does not establish a new acceptance standard.

T/X junctions, bridges, depots, warehouses and combat props are not requested by
this report: the current through-road scene did not require or meaningfully test
them. Fences/hedges and roadside props may be useful, but the inspected evidence
does not yet make them prerequisites. Designer chooses the next bounded step.
