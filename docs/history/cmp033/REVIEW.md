## Findings

The modular architecture is viable: six independent core layers, weapon-owned arms/rifle, common canvas/pivot, per-frame sockets and explicit death-release parts. Equipment variants for Rifleman, Squad Leader, Medic and Engineer appear feasible without replacing every body layer. This is an architectural inference from the delivered parts, not a demonstration of those absent roles. A future runtime adapter must preserve frame synchronization, draw order and the weapon/body ownership contract. No generic framework or role assets were added.

At equal scale the candidate occupies less vertical screen area than the current VIS Soldier: representative standing-walk full alpha extents are about 35×54 source pixels versus the current walk's about 34×78. At .3125 these are approximately 11×17 versus 11×24 screen pixels, including weapon/antialias bounds. The candidate is more explicitly zenith-projected and schematic; current art shows richer material and body definition. The different apparent sizes do not establish a wrong 32 px/m conversion. Do not enlarge the candidate or tilt the camera to match the old silhouette without a design decision.

The sampled four-frame standing cycle changes mainly in small leg/hip regions. At Local zoom the body and rifle are readable as a tiny soldier, but gait/weight transfer is weak and can read as a moving icon. The source and close captures show actual alternating leg poses; this is not a missing-frame or playback bug. Translation remains 1.4 m/s. Walk timing was exercised at .75×/1×/1.25×, corresponding to 1.133/.85/.68 s cycles with all four frames. These timing checks do not establish that a faster setting fixes perceived sliding or improves naturalness. Keep .85 s as the unchanged candidate default. Foot planting is not specified by the source and is not certified from four discrete poses; full live human motion judgement remains necessary. Increasing physical speed is not a correction.

The clearest source correction is the held-rifle left arm. In standing-walk frame 0 its shoulder→elbow is 8.54 px, while elbow→support grip is 22.36 px; the opposite forearm projection is 3.12 px. Across the four frames the left forearm remains 20.01–22.36 px. Aiming foreshortening can cause asymmetry, but these anchors and their actual art produce a long straight bar/triangular support-arm silhouette in the inspected zenith poses. Socket coincidence alone does not prove credible articulation. Measurements are in `evidence/arm_proportions.json`. Correct the pose/arm construction at source while preserving actual grip and shoulder registration.

Visually the candidate is flatter, more diagrammatic and less integrated with the current textured environment than the current Soldier. Its architecture is promising; it is not yet a demonstrated improvement in military presentation. That assessment is a reviewer judgement from the supplied and matched captures, not a claim to know which style Dawson prefers.

Fire/reload can plausibly be authored within the weapon-owned arms/rifle module. Wounded/treatment/interactions can plausibly reuse the core sockets and pose machinery; the existing crouch/prone/death set shows that state-specific geometry is possible. Those additions still require art, timing, ownership and gameplay integration. No fire/reload, treatment, role or combat implementation was requested from the artist here. Directional death selection from blast direction remains future game integration, not supplied behavior.

## Recommended bounded next art request

Before any larger batch, revise only the held-rifle arm articulation and one four-frame standing-walk demonstration. Keep the declared canvas, pivot, scale, north orientation, true overhead projection and movement speed. Preserve the separate weapon/arm ownership. Improve anatomical pose construction and leg/weight-transfer readability through source poses and restrained silhouette/value choices; do not stretch the standing body merely to imitate a tilted projection. Keep four frames initially and show the .85 s default plus any proposed timing alternative separately. Supply the original-package parent hash, new hashes, anchors and Local .3125/close proofs on matching ground at 1.4 m/s. This is a proposal for designer/artist routing, not an instruction already sent to an unconfirmed new artist chat.

Do not commission missing combat states or a full set of role variants until that narrow test and Dawson's intended visual direction are settled. The accepted build and its human-test gate remain unchanged.
