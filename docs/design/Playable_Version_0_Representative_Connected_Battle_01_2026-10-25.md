1. Purpose

This capability assembles the accepted PV0 systems into one ordinary normal-F5 game state that is coherent enough for the first representative Trencho experience evaluation.

It does not create a new logistics, combat, command or outcome model.

Its job is to prove those systems operate together in one player-facing battle.

2. Representative experience

The intended experience is:

operate a protected rear while a real autonomous war unfolds

The player:

acquires physical resources;

lays out the rear;

assigns labor;

builds an Ammunition Workshop;

produces Rifle Ammunition;

chooses when to release it toward the military;

observes the friendly hierarchy use available supply autonomously;

observes an enemy whose logistics are also real but hidden;

sees the battle end only through Combat HQ destruction.

The player never becomes a tactical battlefield commander.

3. Normal F5 is authoritative

The representative scenario is the ordinary normal-F5 entry.

It is not:

a test scene;

a proof fixture;

a prepositioned guard;

a debug-start;

a legacy Battle scene.

Dawson's eventual evaluation uses this normal entry.

6. Representative starting rifle load

For this normal PV0 representative scenario, every ordinary compatible rifle infantry person who previously had a positive normal starting Rifle Ammunition load begins with:

Personnel whose existing authored starting compatible load is 0 remain:

This applies bilaterally.

7. Replenishment targets

The accepted snapshot rule remains unchanged.

Therefore:

ordinary positive-load rifle infantry have replenishment target 2;

existing zero-load personnel have target 0.

Target 0 remains ineligible for this resupply path.

This is a PV0 scenario load, not final carrying capacity.

8. Readiness interaction

Actual firing subsequently creates legitimate readiness pressure.

9. Initial combat separation

The authored representative-sector start must provide a real preparation interval.

Under ordinary untouched objective execution, the earliest legitimate person-versus-person rifle shot must occur:

no earlier than world time 90 seconds

and normally:

no later than world time 120 seconds

No artificial HOLD is used to achieve this.

The interval comes from legitimate starting geography and ordinary movement.

10. Why the preparation interval exists

The player begins with a real rear to operate.

The war is already running, but the player must have enough initial world time to:

receive the first rear shipment;

organize the rear;

build production;

begin preparing military supply.

This is gameplay pacing, not a pause before combat.

76. Scenario decisions
PV0-RCB01-D01 — Two-round normal representative load

Decision: normal positive-target rifle infantry begin with 2 rounds.

Reason: makes physical logistics consequential within the representative battle while preserving D11's exact-threshold ability to begin the objective.

PV0-RCB01-D02 — 90–120 second first-contact window

Decision: representative geography provides an initial rear-operation interval before ordinary rifle contact.

Reason: the player must have meaningful concurrent rear work before battlefield shortage begins.

PV0-RCB01-D03 — 4 Wood / 6 Metal / 2 People acceptance route

Decision: use this legal 20-point first order for the internal representative playthrough.

Reason: it exactly supports workshop construction, four Ammo Crates and two rear workers without debug resources.

Status: evidence route, not forced player order.

PV0-RCB01-D04 — Both manufactured chains must fire in one continuous run

Decision: at least one player-produced and one enemy-produced crate must reach people and be expended before terminal result.

Reason: otherwise #20 would merely place accepted systems beside each other rather than prove they form one game.

PV0-RCB01-D05 — Player shipment authorization control

Decision: a matched control withholds rear shipment confirmation after production.

Reason: proves the player's release decision causally gates military availability.

PV0-RCB01-D06 — Outcome remains emergent

Decision: internal representative run may legitimately end in Friendly Victory or Defeat.

Reason: the player controls logistical possibilities, not the battle result directly.
