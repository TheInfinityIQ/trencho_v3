## Composition and ownership

`sector_layout.gd` generates a 1536x1024m sector at 32 world units/m, with 24 full
256m chunks (6x4). Version `map035-v1`, default seed 35035, alternate 35036.
A private seeded RNG makes all terrain, road, house, tree and spawn decisions.
The canonical complete layout has a SHA-256 diagnostic fingerprint; this is not
a persistence format or cross-engine-version deterministic-simulation promise.

Each chunk holds two 1024-byte arrays for grass variant/rotation, plus indices
into road/house/tree/spawn arrays. Grass tile index implies its exact owner and
8m grid position. All 24576 tiles mix two unchanged Rev6 PNGs with quarter turns.
No terrain tint, seam overlays, per-meter grid or per-tile nodes.

Every generated object has one half-open position-derived chunk owner. A road
canvas extending past the east through-exit belongs to the chunk containing its
visible centerline midpoint. Each moving model/truck also gets a current logical
owner in `actor_chunks`; the initial spawn index is immutable. Actors are hosted
separately from static chunk groups so crossing an ownership boundary cannot hide
them accidentally.

`representative_sector.gd` builds one static Node2D per chunk. MultiMeshInstance2D
batches share source textures and explicit-UV quad meshes by canvas size. Common
z ordering is ground, road, actors, house, canopy, optional debug. Static objects
have no callbacks. Camera region plus a conservative32m margin activates groups;
panning changes visibility without generating data or rebuilding nodes. This is
in-memory culling, not disk streaming, final density or persistence.

## Geometry contracts

Source density remains32px/m, separate from32world units/m (ratio1 here). Rev5
environment and Rev6 grass provenance remain in `art/source_provenance.json`.
Modular Soldier provenance remains `art/modular_soldier_v3/provenance.json`.
No art or source metadata was edited.

Road grammar: west-to-east rural through-road, seeded54–70 eastward straights,
one right/left quarter bend,14–28 vertical straights, opposite bend, eastward
straights to the boundary. No accidental internal termination or new junction.
All placement/orientation comes from layout data. Integer quarter rotations keep
source ports exact. Straight canvas256px=8m; ports (128,0)→(128,255) advance
255px=7.96875m, explicitly overlapping one source pixel at the joint. This obeys
the supplied pixel-index port contract without shrinking/stretching the PNG.

Corner canvas512px=16m, center(511,511), centerline radius311px=9.71875m;
ports(200,511)/(511,200), paved width192px=6m, inner radius215px=6.71875m.
Analytic quarter-circle sampling and endpoint tangents drive both clearance and
the truck route; no diagonal shortcut through a curved tile. Tests pin geometry
constants to the immutable artist metadata and sample the truck's exact supplied
footprint across straights and curves.

Settlement: seeded4–8 houses each side,23m frontage spacing with±1m jitter,
17–23m centerline setbacks. Physical footprint is8x12m, centered on source
(160,218), not the canvas center. South-facing doors on the north row;180°
rotation on the south row puts entrances toward the road. Canvas-center offset
(0,6px) preserves this physical pivot. No nonuniform scaling. Houses do not
overlap. Bare grass yards/access are an exposed composition limitation.

Trees:190–250 targets sampled into7 seeded elliptical groves/woodlines, one near
the village. Minimum road-centerline distance7m, house footprint grown4m, patrol
route clearance2m, trunk-to-trunk spacing1.25m. Source trunk radius12px=.375m;
conservative canvas/canopy envelope radius3m, distinct from trunk collision.
Canopies may overlap. This is bounded placement, not final forestry/collision AI.

## Living scale and cameras

16 genuine `soldier.gd` objects walk deterministic out-and-back routes through
the roadside grass at1.4m/s using existing `advance`. The host renews a completed
route; it is a demonstration patrol, not new tactical AI. `SoldierVisual` remains
the single unchanged read-only renderer, with a model-meter→32world/m transform.
There are no fake visual states, combat, player commands or animation repairs.

One unchanged truck PNG uses source pivot(128,128), physical6x2.4m. It follows
the analytic generated road at provisional12m/s, starting near the village.
At the exit it explicitly wraps to6m inside the entry; no claim of campaign
vehicle behavior, traffic AI or final speed balance. No wrap is hidden by a new
effect. Physics cadence advances models, then samples presentation.

Controls: 1 Local,2 Tactical,3 Sector; WASD/arrows pan; N toggles the two explicit
seeds; Space pauses model/truck updates; F3 toggles details. Buttons do the same.
Local zoom.3125 shows128x80m at1280x800. Sector-specific Tactical.1 shows400x250m,
so a settlement is legible; the SCL full-operation Tactical.025 would nearly show
this entire bounded sector. Sector fits the whole area with margins (~.0202 at
1280x800). Meter conventions and camera clamping reuse the representative-space
concepts; global SCL presets are unchanged. Debug is off by default. Details show
chunk borders, footprints, entrance/trunk marks, routes, coordinates and counts.
