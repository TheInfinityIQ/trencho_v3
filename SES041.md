## Play

Run the project normally (F5): `game_session.tscn` boots into the protected rear. WASD/arrows move YOU. Walk right toward the existing bench, press E for a real manual batch, then Tab or BATTLEFIELD to observe. Return with Tab or REAR. F assigns Worker-01 three finite batches; X stops future batches. Initial four Metal make at most32 rounds; this bounded proof does not replenish them. The worker can finish the remaining three after one manual batch. Manual work holds the avatar until completion, including while observing the battlefield.

Space toggles shared pause/resume. HUD buttons choose Pause, Normal or provisional Fast4x. Battlefield WASD/arrows pan; wheel and1/2/3 retain accepted zoom controls. F3 remains a separate debug-truth display. The old N/new-seed reset is unavailable inside a persistent session. New launches start a new session; view switches do not.

## Ownership and clock

`session_view.gd` is the persistent root, owning one `game_session.gd` and both presentation nodes. GameSession creates the accepted Layout/ENV036 geography, SectorEngagement (AUT038/CNT039) and existing Rear model exactly once; it retains stable session/battle identities. The representative view binds these exact references and does not advance a bound world. Standalone representative proof scenes retain their original self-owned behavior for regression tools.

GameSession is the sole scheduler and authoritative world-time owner: integer ticks at1/60 world second, with one rear and battlefield step per tick. Battlefield's existing `elapsed` and Rear's `world_time` mirror the same progression; neither has a separate live scene timer. Four fixed steps per normal physics tick implement provisional4x, not longer combat ticks. All infantry rates remain1.4m/s of world time; recipe duration remains4 world seconds; contact/report timing is unchanged. Pausing prevents steps and rejects rear production commands. Camera inspection remains usable while paused.

Real-frame deltas are bounded to0.25s and at most60 simulation steps per call. After long stalls the shared world clock slows rather than producing unbounded catch-up; both spaces lose the same wall-time backlog. Engine.time_scale and global SceneTree pause are not used. No off-screen speed multiplier, extra autonomous director or separate rear clock.

View switching changes visibility, camera enablement and presentation/input processing only. It does not free people, reconstruct models, regenerate geography, reset RNG, refill ammo, clear reports, reset time or restart jobs. Battlefield visuals resample retained models on return; hidden presentation work is skipped. Camera position and zoom are retained. Persistent HUD shows only view, world time and rate. Rear UI reads rear-owned stock/work; clean battlefield retains friendly presentation and delivered Company last-known reports, with live enemies/debug truth hidden. No tactical commands or new AI decisions were added.

Existing Rear.advance now accepts no Supply chain for this bounded rear; all existing non-null legacy supply behavior remains. Manufactured stock stays in the real Rear model. No new connection from rear stock to CNT combat ammunition was fabricated: full representative supply-chain integration is explicitly outside SES041. The decorative sector truck remains its documented presentation loop.
